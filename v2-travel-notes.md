# v2 — travelling with the pet

Notes for Samson and his wife. Add anything, in any order. Nothing here is
decided, and nothing here goes near the live app until we agree it does.

## What must stay true

1. The pet never leaves you. In Tabikaeru the frog goes away and you wait.
   Here you go somewhere together, or not at all.
2. Nothing new to do. No streak, no task, no reason to open the app that isn't
   simply wanting to.
3. Money never makes anything happen sooner. Whatever this becomes, it can't
   be waited out or paid past.
4. Travel comes after sitting, never instead of it.

## Seeds (from the first conversation)

- A satchel of places: every mountain you have sat on kept with its cairn, so
  a year of sits becomes a small map you can look back at.
- Postcards: a drawn place and one line, arriving the morning after a sit,
  rather than whenever you ask.
- Seasons: the mountain changes on real dates — first snow, plum blossom,
  the moon in autumn. A reason to come back that isn't a streak.

## Questions worth answering before any code

- Does the travel happen while you sit, or between sits?
- Do you choose where to go, or does it just happen?
- What does the pet do differently in a new place? Anything?
- What would make this feel like more to do? That's the thing to cut.
