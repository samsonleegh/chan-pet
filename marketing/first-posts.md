## 1. Show HN  (news.ycombinator.com/submit) — do this first

**Title**
Show HN: A frog on a mountain you can sit with for five minutes

**URL**
https://shanzhongwa.netlify.app

**First comment** (post it yourself, right after submitting)

I've leaned on meditation and old Zen books at a few anxious points in my
life. I made this so they'd have somewhere to live: a mountain, a rock, a
frog that stays there, and a line to take home after you sit.

Five, fifteen or thirty minutes. A bell at each end. The mountain changes
with the hour on your phone's clock, and you're somewhere new each time you
come back. Afterwards the frog leaves one line in the satchel — Chan and Zen
texts, all public domain, with a plain English reading.

No account, no streaks, no reminders, no notifications, nothing to buy. One
HTML file, works offline, and nothing leaves your phone. There's a dog on
another mountain in there too, with haiku instead.

It's the thing that kept me company, so it's free and it stays free.

## 2. Xiaohongshu 小红书 — for the frog, same day or next

**标题**
一只坐在山里的青蛙，陪你坐五分钟

**正文**

焦虑的那几年，是打坐和几本禅宗的书把我带过来的。
我想给它们一个住的地方，就做了这个。

一座山，一块石头，一只哪儿都不去的青蛙。
坐五分钟、十五分钟、三十分钟，前后各一声钟。
山会跟着手机的时间变颜色，每次回来都在新的地方。
坐完，青蛙会在行囊里留下一句话。

没有账号，没有打卡，没有提醒，没有任何东西要买。
离线可用，什么都不会离开你的手机。
里面还有一只在另一座山上的狗，读的是俳句。

它陪过我，所以它是免费的，以后也是。
